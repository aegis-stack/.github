# Aegis Stack

**Full-stack Python apps that know what they are.**

`uvx aegis-stack init my-app`

A complete FastAPI app, wired and running: API, workers, scheduler, database, auth and AI,
with plugins for anything else. Alongside it is the Twin, a live model of what your app is.
Overseer shows it and lets you operate it, Illiana explains it, and CI catches changes
that break its rules, whether you wrote them or your agents did.

## Apps built on it
- **[Steward](https://github.com/aegis-stack/aegis-steward)**: personal finance on your own server
- **[Aegis Pulse](https://pulse.aegis-stack.io)**: PyPI and GitHub analytics, with reports written from the data

## Plugins
- **[media](https://github.com/aegis-stack/media)**: ffmpeg jobs as named profiles, results in your bucket
- **[hackernews](https://github.com/aegis-stack/hackernews)**: Hacker News as a source for the research service

Build your own: `aegis plugins create`
